Hear My Righteous Plea

A prayer of David.

1Hear, O LORD, my righteous plea;

listen to my cry.

Give ear to my prayer—

it comes from lips free of deceit.

2May my vindication come from YouryourYour presence;

may YouryourYour eyes see what is right.

3You have tried my heart;

You have visited me in the night.

You have tested me and found no evil;

I have resolved not to sin with my mouth.

4As for the deeds of men—

by the word of YouryourYour lips

I have avoided the ways of the violent.

5My steps have held to YouryourYour paths;

my feet have not slipped.

6I call on YouyouYou, O God,

for YouyouYou will answer me.

Incline YouryourYour ear to me;

hear my words.

7Show the wonders of YouryourYour loving devotion,

You who save by YouryourYour right hand

those who seek refuge from their foes.

8Keep me as the apple ofa YouryourYour eye;

hide me in the shadow of YouryourYour wings

9from the wicked who assail me,

from my mortal enemies who surround me.

10They have closed their callous hearts;

their mouths speak with arrogance.

11They have tracked us down, and now surround us;

their eyes are set to cast us to the ground,

12like a lion greedy for prey,

like a young lion lurking in ambush.

13Arise, O LORD, confront them!

Bring them to their knees;

deliver me from the wicked by YouryourYour sword,

14from such men, O LORD, by YouryourYour hand—

from men of the world

whose portion is in this life.

May YouyouYou fill the bellies of YouryourYour treasured onesb and satisfy their sons,

so they leave their abundance to their children.

15As for me, I will behold YouryourYour face in righteousness;

when I awake, I will be satisfied in YouryourYour presence.
