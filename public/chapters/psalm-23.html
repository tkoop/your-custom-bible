The LORD Is My Shepherd
(Ezekiel 34:11–24; John 10:1–21)

A Psalm of David.

1The LORD is my shepherd;a

I shall not want.

2He makes me lie down in green pastures;

HeheHe leads me beside quiet waters.

3He restores my soul;

HeheHe guides me in the paths of righteousness

for the sake of HishisHis name.

4Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death,b

I will fear no evil,

for YouyouYou are with me;

YouryourYour rod and YouryourYour staff, they comfort me.

5You prepare a table before me

in the presence of my enemies.

You anoint my head with oil;

my cup overflows.

6Surely goodness and mercy will follow me

all the days of my life,

and I will dwell in the house of the LORD

forever.
