BOOK IV

Psalms 90–106

From Everlasting to Everlasting

A prayer of Moses the man of God.

1Lord, YouyouYou have been our dwelling place

through all generations.

2Before the mountains were born

or YouyouYou brought forth the earth and the world,

from everlasting to everlasting

You are God.

3You return man to dust,

saying, “Return, O sons of mortals.”

4For in YouryourYour sight a thousand years

are but a day that passes,a

or a watch of the night.

5You whisk them away in their sleep;

they are like the new grass of the morning—

6in the morning it springs up new,

but by evening it fades and withers.

7For we are consumed by YouryourYour anger

and terrified by YouryourYour wrath.

8You have set our iniquities before YouyouYou,

our secret sins in the light of YouryourYour presence.

9For all our days decline in YouryourYour fury;

we finish our years with a sigh.

10The length of our days is seventy years—

or eighty if we are strong—

yet their pride is but labourlabourlabor and sorrow,

for they quickly pass, and we fly away.

11Who knows the power of YouryourYour anger?

Your wrath matches the fear YouyouYou are due.

12So teach us to number our days,

that we may present a heart of wisdom.

13Return, O LORD! How long will it be?

Have compassion on YouryourYour servants.

14Satisfy us in the morning with YouryourYour loving devotion,

that we may sing for joy and be glad all our days.

15Make us glad for as many days as YouyouYou have afflicted us,

for as many years as we have seen evil.

16May YouryourYour work be shown to YouryourYour servants,

and YouryourYour splendoursplendoursplendor to their children.

17May the favourfavourfavorb of the Lord our God rest upon us;

establish for us the work of our hands—

yes, establish the work of our hands!
