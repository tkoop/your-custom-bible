Create in Me a Clean Heart, O God
(2 Samuel 12:1–12)

For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. When Nathan the prophet came to him after his adultery with Bathsheba.

1Have mercy on me,a O God,

according to YouryourYour loving devotion;

according to YouryourYour great compassion,

blot out my transgressions.

2Wash me clean of my iniquity

and cleanse me from my sin.

3For I know my transgressions,

and my sin is always before me.

4Against YouyouYou, YouyouYou only, have I sinned

and done what is evil in YouryourYour sight,

so that YouyouYou may be proved right when YouyouYou speak

and blameless when YouyouYou judge.b

5Surely I was brought forth in iniquity;

I was sinful when my mother conceived me.

6Surely YouyouYou desire truth in the inmost being;

YouyouYou teach me wisdom in the inmost place.

7Purify me with hyssop, and I will be clean;

wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.

8Let me hear joy and gladness;

let the bones YouyouYou have crushed rejoice.

9Hide YouryourYour face from my sins

and blot out all my iniquities.

10Create in me a clean heart, O God,

and renew a right spirit within me.

11Cast me not away from YouryourYour presence;

take not YouryourYour HolyholyHoly SpiritspiritSpirit from me.

12Restore to me the joy of YouryourYour salvation,

and sustain me with a willing spirit.

13Then I will teach transgressors YouryourYour ways,

and sinners will return to YouyouYou.

14Deliver me from bloodguilt, O God,

the God of my salvation,

and my tongue will sing of YouryourYour righteousness.

15O Lord, open my lips,

and my mouth will declare YouryourYour praise.

16For YouyouYou do not delight in sacrifice, or I would bring it;

YouyouYou take no pleasure in burnt offerings.

17The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit;

a broken and a contrite heart,

O God, YouyouYou will not despise.

18In YouryourYour good pleasure, cause Zion to prosper;

build up the walls of Jerusalem.

19Then YouyouYou will delight in righteous sacrifices,

in whole burnt offerings;

then bulls will be offered on YouryourYour altar.
