BOOK II

Psalms 42–72

As the Deer Pants for the Water

For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah.a

1As the deer pants for streams of water,

so my soul longs after YouyouYou, O God.

2My soul thirsts for God, the living God.

When shall I come and appear in God’s presence?b

3My tears have been my food

both day and night,

while men ask me all day long,

“Where is your God?”

4These things come to mind as I pour out my soul:

how I walked with the multitude,

leading the procession to the house of God

with shouts of joy and praise.

5Why are you downcast, O my soul?

Why the unease within me?

Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise HimhimHim

for the salvation of HishisHis presence.

6O my God, my soul despairs within me.

Therefore I remember YouyouYou

from the land of Jordan and the peaks of Hermon—

even from Mount Mizar.

7Deep calls to deep

in the roar of YouryourYour waterfalls;

all YouryourYour breakers and waves

have rolled over me.

8The LORD decrees HishisHis loving devotion by day,

and at night HishisHis song is with me

as a prayer to the God of my life.

9I say to God my RockrockRock,

“Why have YouyouYou forgotten me?

Why must I walk in sorrow

because of the enemy’s oppression?”

10Like the crushing of my bones,

my enemies taunt me,

while they say to me all day long,

“Where is your God?”

11Why are you downcast, O my soul?

Why the unease within me?
