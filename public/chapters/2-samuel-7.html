God’s Covenant with David
(1 Chronicles 17:1–15)

1After the king had settled into his palace and the LORD had given him rest from all his enemies around him, 2he said to Nathan the prophet, “Here I am, living in a house of cedar, while the ark of God remains in a tent.”

3And Nathan replied to the king, “Go and do all that is in your heart, for the LORD is with you.”

4But that night the word of the LORD came to Nathan, saying, 5“Go and tell MymyMy servant David that this is what the LORD says: Are you the one to build for MemeMe a house to dwell in? 6For I have not dwelt in a house from the day I brought the Israelites up out of Egypt until this day, but I have moved about with a tent as MymyMy dwelling. 7In all MymyMy journeys with all the Israelites, have I ever asked any of the leadersa I appointed to shepherd MymyMy people Israel, ‘Why haven’t you built MemeMe a house of cedar?’

Now I will make for you a name like the greatest in the land.

10And I will provide a place for MymyMy people Israel and will plant them so that they may dwell in a place of their own and be disturbed no more. No longer will the sons of wickedness oppress them as they did at the beginning 11and have done since the day I appointed judges over MymyMy people Israel. 17So Nathan relayed to David all the words of this entire vision.
