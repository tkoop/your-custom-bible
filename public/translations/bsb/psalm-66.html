Make a Joyful Noise
(Psalm 100:1–5)

For the choirmaster. A song. A Psalm.

1Make a joyful noise to God,

all the earth!

2Sing the glory of HishisHis name;

make HishisHis praise glorious.

3Say to God, “How awesome are YouryourYour deeds!

So great is YouryourYour power

that YouryourYour enemies cower before YouyouYou.

4All the earth bows down to YouyouYou;

they sing praise to YouyouYou;

they sing praise to YouryourYour name.”

Selah

5Come and see the works of God;

how awesome are HishisHis deeds toward mankind.

6He turned the sea into dry land;

they passed through the waters on foot;

there we rejoiced in HimhimHim.

7He rules forever by HishisHis power;

HishisHis eyes watch the nations.

Do not let the rebellious exalt themselves.

Selah

8Bless our God, O peoples;

let the sound of HishisHis praise be heard.

9He preserves our lives

and keeps our feet from slipping.

10For YouyouYou, O God, have tested us;

YouyouYou have refined us like silver.

11You led us into the net;

YouyouYou laid burdens on our backs.

12You let men ride over our heads;

we went through fire and water,

but YouyouYou brought us into abundance.

13I will enter YouryourYour house with burnt offerings;

I will fulfillfulfilfulfill my vows to YouyouYou—

14the vows that my lips promised

and my mouth spoke in my distress.

15I will offer YouyouYou fatlings as burnt offerings,

with the fragrant smoke of rams;

I will offer bulls and goats.

Selah

16Come and listen, all you who fear God,

and I will declare what HeheHe has done for me.

17I cried out to HimhimHim with my mouth

and praised HimhimHim with my tongue.a

18If I had cherished iniquity in my heart,

the Lord would not have listened.

19But God has surely heard;

HeheHe has attended to the sound of my prayer.

20Blessed be God, who has not rejected my prayer

or withheld from me HishisHis loving devotion!
